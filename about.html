Our Community Role

Serving the Healthcare Needs of Lalamusa

Umer Din Pharmacy & Surgical is an established community retail pharmacy located directly on Main G.T. Road, next to the Bank of Punjab in Lalamusa. We serve local families, home caregivers, and neighborhood healthcare clinics with dependable daily access to authentic medicines and medical supplies.

Our dispensary stocks genuine, DRAP-registered prescription medicines sourced exclusively from authorized pharmaceutical distributors. We maintain strict storage controls, including continuous commercial refrigeration for cold-chain insulins, vaccines, and sensitive biological treatments to protect medicine potency.

Alongside prescription care, we provide essential patient recovery equipment, diagnostic monitoring devices, infant nutrition, and personal skincare. Customers can visit our pharmacy in person or connect through WhatsApp for prompt availability checks and local doorstep delivery within Lalamusa.
