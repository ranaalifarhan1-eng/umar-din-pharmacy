Who We Are

Serving the Healthcare Needs of Lalamusa

Umer Din Pharmacy & Surgical is a community retail pharmacy situated directly on Main G.T. Road, next to the Bank of Punjab in Lalamusa. We serve local families, home caregivers, and neighborhood healthcare clinics across the city and nearby localities with reliable access to daily medicines and medical supplies.

Our dispensary focuses on stocking genuine, DRAP-registered prescription medicines and healthcare products procured from authorized pharmaceutical distributors. We maintain careful storage standards, including continuous commercial refrigeration for cold-chain insulins, vaccines, and sensitive biological treatments to protect medicine potency.

Alongside prescription pharmaceuticals, we provide a practical range of surgical and home patient-care equipment, diagnostic monitoring devices, infant nutrition, and personal skincare items. Customers can visit our pharmacy in person or reach out through WhatsApp for prompt medicine availability checks, prescription inquiries, and local doorstep delivery within Lalamusa.
